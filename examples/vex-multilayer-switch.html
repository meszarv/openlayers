Each vector layer hosts ~20 randomized points with unique styling so you can easily spot redraw issues or ghosting artifacts as you pan and zoom. The automatic mode keeps Vex active while zoomed out, then lets the shared canvas renderer take over when crossing window.vexRendererSwitchZoom.

Toggle between the two modes to verify that pooled canvases stay responsive and that the renderer switch happens seamlessly across hundreds of layers.
